Who is Eliot B?

Hello! My name is Eliot, and I am currently a Computer Science student. I love learning about new technology and understanding how computers work. I hope to one day bring my technical skills to the private sector and work for defense contractors such as Lockheed Martin!

Aside from computer science, I am also a cadet in the crosstown AFROTC program at WSU! I am in my third year which means I get to teach and mentor the younger classes. It has been both very challenging and very rewarding. I will find out if I get a pilot slot at the end of the spring semester.

Outside of the school, I love to be in the outdoors. I like to hunt, fish, snowboard, hike, and go shooting with my friends. I like to live an active lifestyle which keeps me grounded and healthy.
