About the Project

FightingAce is a passion project of mine which exposed me to both game development and iOS app development. In the game you control a fighter aircraft from a top-down view and have full control of direction, speed, and your munitions. The aim of the game is to destroy enemy aircraft which are programmed to hunt and kill you. Your score goes up depending on how many kills you are able to achieve!

The game is written entirely in Swift which was a simple yet robust langauge that exposed me to many game development principles. I was able to implement randomized terrain, AI models for the enemy aircraft, and missile locking and projectile physics that make the game highly interactice and realistic! I haven't deployed the game to the app store yet due to me not carrying an Apple Developer License, but my plan is to complete it and deploy it within the next year!

Stay tuned for more information and announcements on updates and its release!
